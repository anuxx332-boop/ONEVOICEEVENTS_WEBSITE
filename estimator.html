Frequently Asked Questions on Pricing

Are travel and generator fuel costs included in these estimates?

For events within Kegalle district, transport is fully included. For outstation venues (Colombo, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Galle), minimal standard transport overhead is added based on truck logistics.

Can we swap items between packages?

Yes, absolutely! You can choose a Gold sound system with a Silver catering tier or add extra Cold Pyro fountains. Click any WhatsApp button to tell Lochana and Anusara your custom preferences.

How do we lock our date on the calendar?

We require a standard 25% advance confirmation deposit to reserve all audio-visual gear, technicians, and date availability. The remaining balance is payable on event day.
